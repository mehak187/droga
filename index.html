Who We Are

Droga Industries is a leading custom apparel manufacturer with over 25 years of expertise in creating exceptional fashion garments. We are a trusted manufacturer specializing in premium Fashion Wear, Sportswear, Formal Wear & Swimwear, serving global brands through platforms like Alibaba with unmatched quality, reliability, and ethical manufacturing practices that make a positive impact on communities worldwide.

Our Mission

To revolutionize the fashion industry by delivering world-class manufacturing solutions that empower brands globally while creating positive social impact. As a responsible manufacturer, we bridge the gap between premium quality and affordability, ensuring every piece of Fashion Wear, Sportswear, Formal Wear & Swimwear exceeds international standards while maintaining competitive pricing, timely delivery, and contributing to community welfare through our ethical business practices.

Our Promise

Excellence in every thread, integrity in every partnership, and positive impact in every community we touch. As a certified manufacturer, we guarantee premium quality Fashion Wear, Sportswear, Formal Wear & Swimwear with 16+ international certifications, competitive pricing, and ethical manufacturing practices. Our commitment extends beyond business - we contribute 4% of profits to community welfare and environmental sustainability, making every garment a catalyst for positive change and social responsibility.
